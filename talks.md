  <h2 class="talk__title">VulnScopper: Unveiling Hidden Links Between Unseen Security Entities</h2>
  <p class="talk__desc">
    The NVD announced it could no longer fully enrich new CVEs at the pace they arrive.
    VulnScopper combines knowledge graphs and LLMs - using a GNN foundation model (ULTRA)
    in an inductive setup - to automate vulnerability enrichment and link CVEs to related
    security entities, including ones the model has not seen at training time.
  </p>
  <p class="talk__desc">
    Authors: Daniel Alfasi, Tal Shapira, Anat Bremler-Barr.
    Published in <em>Proceedings of the 3rd GNNet Workshop on Graph Neural Networking</em>,
    pages 33-40.
  </p>
